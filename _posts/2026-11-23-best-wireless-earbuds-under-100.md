## How to choose

- **Noise canceling (ANC):** worth it if you commute or fly. "Adaptive" ANC adjusts to your surroundings automatically.
- **Fit matters more than specs:** the best-sounding earbud is the one that seals well in *your* ears. Try every ear-tip size in the box.
- **Codecs:** iPhones use AAC; many Android phones also support LDAC or aptX for higher-quality Bluetooth audio. Check that your phone supports a codec before paying for it.
- **Battery:** look for at least 6–8 hours per charge with ANC on.
- **App and EQ:** a good app lets you fix a too-bassy or too-bright sound in seconds.

## Bottom line

For most people, the **EarFun Air Pro 4+** is the best all-rounder under $100. If blocking noise is your top priority, get the **Soundcore Space A40**.
