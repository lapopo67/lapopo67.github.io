## How to choose

- **Capacity:** 5,000mAh is roughly one phone charge; 10,000mAh about two; 20,000mAh+ is for multi-day trips or tablets and laptops. Real-world output is lower than the label because of conversion losses.
- **Wattage:** 20–30W fast-charges most phones; laptops usually need 45–100W from a single port.
- **Ports:** USB-C in *and* out is the modern standard. A built-in cable means one less thing to forget.
- **Flying:** power banks must go in carry-on luggage, not checked bags. Packs under 100Wh are generally allowed without airline approval; check your airline's rules.
- **Safety:** stick to established brands and avoid banks that are swollen, damaged or get unusually hot.
